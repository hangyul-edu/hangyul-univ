# V8 research protocol — Students

> **Status: protocol only — NOT RUN.** No sessions with real students have taken place. Nothing in REPORT §52–54 or the
> scorecard is based on this study. Task timings in REPORT §53 come from scripted walkthroughs by the build team and are
> labelled as such. Results go into `docs/research/v8-student-results.md` only after real sessions.

## 1. Questions we need answered
1. In the first session, does a student understand where they stand (programs they meet / one step away / "we need your grades to check")? Do they ever read missing data as rejection?
2. Can a student shortlist, compare and judge the first-year cost (Hangyul fee and cash before arrival included) without help?
3. Is the stage-aware navigation found where expected, especially after admission (visa) and after the visa (arrival)?
4. Does "upload once, reuse" stay true in the student's mental model: do they re-upload anyway?
5. Is the writing loop (draft → reviewer comments → accept/decline → next version) clearer than Word plus chat?

## 2. Participants
- 10 students in 2 rounds of 5 (Nielsen's five-user rounds, fix between rounds).
- Mix: ≥ 3 Vietnamese, ≥ 2 Uzbek, ≥ 2 Nepali or Burmese, ≥ 1 Mongolian. ≥ 4 on a phone only. ≥ 3 use a non-English UI language (vi / uz / ne / my).
- Stage mix: 4 exploring, 3 preparing applications, 3 admitted or in visa preparation.
- Excluded: Hangyul staff, agency staff, relatives of staff.
- Recruitment: partner agencies' opt-in lists and student communities. No pressure from a counselor in the session.
- Incentive: a fixed voucher, independent of what they say.

## 3. Consent and privacy
- Written consent in the participant's language. Covers recording of screen and voice, storage for 12 months, and withdrawal at any time.
- Test accounts with synthetic data only. Participants never enter their real passport, documents or grades.
- Recordings stay on Hangyul-owned storage and are never shared with agencies or universities.

## 4. Tasks (think-aloud, 45 min, moderated remotely or in person)
| # | Task | Success criterion | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "Find three programs you could apply to for spring." (exploring persona, no grades entered) | Names ≥ 3 programs from "meets" or "one step away"; correctly explains a "needs your grades" item | time, errors, misreading of missing data as ineligibility (yes/no) |
| T2 | "Which of your saved programs costs least in the first year, including what you pay before you fly?" | Correct program and the amount before arrival | time, number of pages visited |
| T3 | "What does Hangyul itself cost you?" | Finds the fee on the budget or services page | time, success |
| T4 | "Your counselor says your graduation certificate needs a new copy. Fix it." | Replaces the document once; does not re-upload elsewhere | time, re-uploads |
| T5 | "Read your reviewer's comments and make a new version." | Accepts/declines ≥ 1 comment, submits a new version | time, confusion points |
| T6 (admitted persona) | "What do you need to do next for your visa?" | Opens the visa checklist from the nav and names the next document | time, nav path |
| T7 (arrived persona) | "You lost your residence card. Where do you get help?" | Reaches Help, sees the emergency numbers and the case form | time |

## 5. Measures
- Task success (complete / with help / fail), time on task, number of navigation steps, SEQ (1–7) after each task.
- SUS at the end. One open question: "What would you tell a friend about this?"
- Critical-incident log: any moment the participant believes something false, such as "I'm not eligible" or "they will submit for me".

## 6. Analysis
- Issues are rated by severity (0–4, Nielsen) × frequency. Everything rated ≥ 3 is fixed before round 2.
- Results go into the results file with participant codes only. Any product change based on them is logged in REPORT with the participant evidence.
