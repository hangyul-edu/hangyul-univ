# V8 research protocol — Parents and sponsors (evaluation of a parent view)

> **Status: protocol only — NOT RUN.** V8 does **not** build a parent view (REPORT §52). The decision rests on the reasoning
> below; this study is what would justify building one.

## 1. Why a parent view is not built in V8
- **What a parent needs from Hangyul:** the first-year cost and cash before arrival, the deadline for payment, the offer and scholarship, and the safety of arrival (pickup, housing). The budget page (with Hangyul's fee since V8), the offer decision ("You pay ₩X by …") and the arrival checklist already show all of this to the student.
- **Risk:** a separate parent login creates consent and privacy questions. Students are adults; their documents, grades and messages are theirs. It also creates a second support channel, and the agency may start addressing the parent instead of the student.
- **Cheaper alternative:** a read-only, student-initiated share of the budget/offer summary (a link that expires, with no documents or messages). This is listed in ROADMAP as a candidate. It will be built only if this study shows that parents pay and decide, and that students want to share.

## 2. Questions
1. Who decides on, and who pays, the tuition and Hangyul fee? (By country: VN, UZ, NP, MN, MM.)
2. What do parents ask the student or the agency for today, and through which channel?
3. Would students share a read-only cost/offer summary with their parents? What must never be shared?

## 3. Participants
- 8 parent–student pairs (interviewed separately, then together), ≥ 2 each from VN and UZ, and ≥ 1 each from NP, MN and MM.
- Interviews in the parent's language with an interpreter.

## 4. Method
- 30-min semi-structured interview with each parent, then a 15-min concept test of a printed mock of the shared summary.
- Measures: who pays and who decides (coded), the information parents ask for (affinity map), the student's comfort with sharing (1–7) and the items they veto.

## 5. Decision rule
Build the share link only if ≥ 5 of 8 families say the parent is the main payer **and** ≥ 6 of 8 students rate their comfort with sharing ≥ 5.
