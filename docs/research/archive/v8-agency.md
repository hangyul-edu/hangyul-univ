# V8 research protocol — Agency counselors and managers

> **Status: protocol only — NOT RUN.** No sessions with real agency staff have taken place. The V7 → V8 measurements in
> REPORT §53 are scripted walkthroughs by the build team, not observations of counselors.

## 1. Questions
1. Can a counselor start the day and name the 10 most urgent cases, and why each is urgent, from Today alone?
2. Do the priority reasons ("Deadline in 2 days · documents missing") match the counselor's own judgement? Where do they disagree?
3. Are the document and essay review loops faster than their current tools (email, KakaoTalk, Google Drive)?
4. Can a manager find, in under 60 s each, the oldest blocked case, an overloaded counselor, SLA failures, deadline risk and the slowest stage?
5. Is the "Not connected" state of external messaging channels understood, with no expectation of WhatsApp/Zalo delivery?

## 2. Participants
- 6 counselors (≥ 2 in Vietnam, ≥ 1 in Uzbekistan, ≥ 1 in Nepal) and 3 managers from at least 3 different agencies.
- ≥ 1 year of work on Korean admissions. Mix of large agencies (> 200 students a year) and small ones.
- Sessions run in the participant's working language, with an interpreter where needed.

## 3. Consent and data
- Written consent. Seeded demo tenant with synthetic students only. No real student data enters the session.
- Agency names are not recorded in results; participants are coded C1–C6 and M1–M3.

## 4. Tasks (60 min, moderated)
| # | Role | Task | Success | Measures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | counselor | "Start your day: which students need you first, and why?" | Lists the top 5 and gives each one's reason | time, agreement with the shown order (Kendall τ vs. the participant's own ranking) |
| A2 | counselor | "Review the documents waiting for you." | Clears ≥ 3 with a correct decision | time per document, navigation steps |
| A3 | counselor | "Give feedback on Minh's study plan." | Leaves ≥ 2 comments and requests a revision | time, steps |
| A4 | counselor | "Which students have been waiting on you for more than 3 days?" | Uses the saved view and gives the count | time |
| A5 | counselor | "Send Lan a reminder on WhatsApp." | Recognises that WhatsApp is not connected and uses in-app messaging | misunderstanding (yes/no) |
| A6 | manager | Five questions: oldest blocked case, overloaded counselor, SLA failures, deadline risk, slowest stage | Each answered correctly in < 60 s | time per question |

## 5. Measures and analysis
- Success, time, steps, SEQ, and agreement between the priority order and the participant's own.
- Severity × frequency for issues.
- Before/after comparison against the same tasks performed in the participant's current toolset (self-timed diary, 1 day).
