## 2. Key findings (≤20)

1. **Korea will make the agency visible on the visa document itself.** The MOJ's 2026-10-07 package schedules **"표준입학허가서에 유학원 정보 필수 입력"** (agency details mandatory on the Certificate of Admission, CoA) for H2-2026. It also plans a **"해외 유학원 등록제"** (overseas agency registration with Korean missions) by H2-2027, with a 5-mission pilot, mandatory visa-policy training for registered agencies and local action against illegal brokers [S1][S2][S3]. The stated reasons are "과도한 수수료, 부정확한 정보 제공, 허위서류 작성" (excessive fees, inaccurate information, false documents) [S2]. The UK runs the same pattern: UKVI adds agent information to the CAS [S72]. *Status: draft plan ("안"); dates are targets.*
2. **Universities will be scored on their visa-refusal rate.** The MOJ plans a "대학별 비자 관리 역량 평가" (university visa-management assessment) covering visa refusal rate, graduation, employment, Korean-language attainment and the domestic-student ratio. The result feeds into visa screening [S3]. This adds to the existing illegal-stay thresholds, where certification needs under 1–2% (degree) and the survey floor is 5% [S5]. **A university's agency choices now show up directly in its own visa outcomes.**
3. **Financial proof is being split by Korean ability.** Applicants with TOPIK 4 or KIIP 4 will be exempt from financial documents (H2-2026). Applicants without Korean from "중점관리" (high-risk) countries will be moved onto a **study-expense deposit scheme** from H1-2027: money is held in a bank and released in tranches each semester on proof of enrolment [S1][S2][S3]. A single "bank balance ≥ X" checklist will no longer be correct for every student.
4. **The CoA pipeline is becoming digital on the government side.** The MOJ plans a FIMS university menu with **bulk CoA generation**, visit-free and paper-free online visa filings, and a "체류 이력 통합 프로필" (integrated stay-history profile) [S3]. Today, CoAs are e-mailed one by one, take "up to 10 days", and students are told not to ask about individual dates [S22].
5. **The standard university–agency agreement (October 2026) is not yet public.** MOE announced it on 2026-09-08 [S6–S11]: no false advertising, agents must pass on information universities request, refund rules disclosed in advance, and tuition refunded "directly to an account in the student's name" after a visa denial [S7][S11]. A Higher Education Act amendment would make it binding [S7]. *We found no published text as of 2026-10-10.*
6. **Refund failures are now a documented, political problem.** There were 115 complaints in 5 years, 90 of them to MOE in 2026 alone. Singyeongju Univ. has about 400 students with KRW 2.8bn delayed, and Woongji College about 170 students with KRW 0.9bn. The ministry "lacks an enforcement mechanism" [S14]. University guides refund only to Korean accounts, or to the student's own account [S21][S20].
7. **Document fraud in the Korean pipeline is industrial, not anecdotal:**
   - more than 100 fabricated US degrees used for D-2 transfers at Honam Univ. (search, then the students left Korea) [S15];
   - a TOPIK proxy ring with 113 arrests, recruiting on Xiaohongshu, with scores used for admission and scholarships [S16];
   - a Telegram broker who sold forged leases to 133 students (59 deported or notified), caught because "similar leases" kept appearing [S17];
   - temporary deposits made only to obtain a balance certificate, ruled "unlawful means" (대법원 2024도14827) [S18].
8. **Payment rails create fraud risk of their own.** KU warns that tuition sent through illegal money changers or payment proxies can get the student's **virtual account frozen** [S20]. PNU says that exchanging money outside banks "can stop your bank account" [S23]. Agencies that collect and forward tuition are exactly this pattern (**inference**).
9. **Vietnam joined the Apostille Convention on 2026-09-11** [S38][S39]. Thailand follows on 2027-02-28 [S38]. Many Korean guides still list "consular confirmation" or CHSI for these routes [S21][S19]. Per-country document rules are now out of date in many agency checklists (**inference**). HCCH marks Viet Nam "A**" (some state objected); whether Korea objected is unconfirmed and needs checking before any rule changes.
10. **Korean rails still have no agency path, while UCAS does.** UCAS lets an international agent register free as a centre, using a member-university reference, and "manage and submit applications through the adviser portal" [S69]. Korea is the opposite:
    - KU says applications "must be made personally by each applicant" [S19];
    - JinhakApply forbids third parties using an ID (Art. 16(2), 26(1)(4)) [S25];
    - UWAY and Jinhak public pages show no agency, batch or API features [S26][S27].
11. **Admission offices run on e-mail, post, Google Forms and virtual accounts.**
    - Sookmyung: copies by e-mail, then originals by post [S22].
    - PNU: dormitory applications via `forms.gle`; separate virtual accounts per fee item; overseas wires at a fixed KRW 1,400/USD plus USD 100 with "about 1 week" before confirmation; nine separate team e-mail addresses [S23].
    - Seojeong: originals in person, three rounds, Shinhan virtual accounts [S21].
12. **Payments are bridged by hand.** At Inha, the applicant e-mails their UWAY ID, staff send a Flywire invoice link, and the UWAY exam number appears after payment [S29]. Yonsei Flywire opens to *new* students only in 2027 and settles in about 3–5 business days [S30].
13. **Immigration is coming to campus.** Ajou registered 418 students in one on-campus visit [S34]. Busan runs 25+ sessions at 21 universities for about 7,000 students [S35]. SNU's second group round is slow [S36]. Residence-card timing now depends on **which university batch the student joins**, not only on the 90-day rule (**inference** from [S34–S36]).
14. **Agency money flows are slow and reconciled by hand.**
    - Commission typically arrives 3–6 months after enrolment.
    - One university reconciles students against invoices in Excel.
    - Duplicate agent claims happen in fewer than 5% of cases; the agent is locked once the deposit is paid [S51].
    - 67% of US institutions pay only after all fees are received [S52].

    No source covers Korean universities' commission timing (*no public evidence*).
15. **Agency CRMs sell pipelines, not operations.** Agentcis lists lead forms, a document checklist, a client portal, "subagents", invoicing and WhatsApp campaigns. It names no assignment, SLA or ageing features [S49]. Meritto's pitch names the real pains: lead leakage, multi-channel chaos and repetitive manual work [S50]. Neither models Korean rules.
16. **Global marketplaces compete on speed, and the claims drift.**
    - Leverage now advertises "One-Day" submission, "One-Week" offer and **"One-Month" payout** (for agents) [S62].
    - ApplyBoard advertises a "95% chance of application success" [S55].
    - IDP's landing page says "instant offer", while its partner terms say "indicative… expires within 30 days… cancelled" if documents mismatch [S56][S57].
17. **Education finance for non-US destinations is thin.** MPOWER has "reached our current funding capacity" for 2026 and covers only US and Canadian schools [S66]. No lender found covers Korea.
18. **Parent and sponsor visibility is almost absent everywhere.** The only agency evidence is Crimson's meeting summaries sent to parents (*excerpt*) [S76]. Korean universities treat guardians as an emergency contact [S19]. Yonsei *penalises* personal statements that name parents' jobs or status [S24]. Parents pay by wire or Alipay to virtual accounts, with no shared status view (**inference** from [S19][S20][S23]).
19. **Regulators elsewhere already measure agents.** UK AQF partners propose sharing visa-refusal, retention and continuation data per agent [S72]. Australia banned commissions on onshore transfers from 2026-03-31 [S70]. Canada has verified every letter of acceptance with the institution since 2023-12 (1,813 flagged by 2024-01) [S73]. Korea's plan is converging on the same three levers: identify the agent, measure outcomes, verify documents [S1–S3].
20. **The new Korea-specific players are still point solutions.** YesFuture/VIVISA does visa and settlement (B2C, plus a B2B "VIVISA Uni") [S47]. HigherVisa does stay administration [S32]. Go! Go! Hanguk is a free liaison that answers by e-mail in 1–3 business days [S46]. The government fairs call themselves a "public study-abroad platform" [S48]. **No one connects admission → CoA → visa → arrival → retention on one case record.**

Legend for the failure-mode column:
- **CP** copy-paste
- **RU** re-upload
- **RM** manual reminder
- **XL** Excel/Sheets

## 8. Do not copy (dark patterns found in this pass)

1. **Speed guarantees as the headline:** Leverage "One-Day / One-Week / One-Month payout" [S62]; IDP "instant offer" without a non-binding label on the landing page, while the terms say "indicative… cancelled" [S56][S57].
2. **Unexplained success rates:** ApplyBoard "95% chance of application success" [S55].
3. **Undisclosed fees:** no service fee shown (Leverage) [S62]. "Free" services with no explanation of who pays (Go! Go! Hanguk says "no more than applying directly" but doesn't say whether the school pays it) [S46].
4. **Unverifiable "100% verified" badges and inconsistent counters:** UniAcco "100% verified listings" and 500+ vs 700+ cities on one page [S67].
5. **Contradictory fees on one listing:** Trazy waiting fee 20k/30 min vs 15k/h, plus two late-night charges [S43].

12. **"Don't ask us" as a status policy** [S22]: the opposite of what Hangyul should do.

## 9. Implications for Hangyul: concrete removals

*Each item names what is removed (step, duplicate, decision, tool switch, uncertainty or error) and the evidence. Product design is out of scope; these are research recommendations.*

**Remove duplication**
1. **One document vault, many destinations.** Today the same transcript or bank certificate is uploaded to each rail, posted, re-presented at the embassy, at the bank and for the residence card [S19][S23][S40]. Store each document once with its issue date, expiry and authentication route, then show where it has already been used. *Removes RU across stages 7, 19, 29, 38 and 40.*
2. **One arrival record.** The flight number is re-typed into pickup, shuttle and dorm check-in [S43][S44]; the Korean address and phone are re-typed into the university SIS [S23]. *Removes CP at stages 33–41.*
3. **Prefill, never submit.** Generate a "what to type" sheet per rail from the profile (V6 guided hand-off). Keep the student as the submitter [S19][S25]. *Removes CP at stage 17 without breaching the rules.*

**Remove uncertainty**

4. **Validity clocks on every document.** Bank certificates must be issued within 30 days and are valid up to 6 months [S22]; there are per-country variants [S22][S23]. Warn before expiry; recompute when the target university changes. *Removes MD at stage 8.*
5. **Per-country authentication rules with an effective date.** Vietnam moved to Apostille on 2026-09-11 and Thailand moves on 2027-02-28; Nepal still needs consular confirmation [S38][S39]. Store `route`, `effective_from` and `source`; flag university guides that still demand the old route. *Removes CF at stage 12.*
6. **Show the university's tier and its visa consequence.** Excellent, certified, general, consulting, banned. What changes for *this* nationality (CoA-only vs financial proof) [S5]. *Removes the qualification decision at stage 3.*
7. **Explain the CoA sequence per university.** Some universities need tuition first and some don't [S20][S21]; issuance takes up to 10 days [S22]. Give a dated expectation instead of "don't ask". *Removes uncertainty at stage 26.*
8. **Treat visa refusal as a refund trigger.** On refusal, start the refund case automatically: the university's refund ladder, Korean-account rule, POA option and FX cost [S7][S21]. Show elapsed days, because complaints show delays [S14]. *Removes the refund–status CF at stages 31 and 46.*

**Remove decisions the student shouldn't have to make**

9. **Payment-route guard.** Only show bank, Flywire or university-listed methods. Warn explicitly against money changers and proxies, citing the frozen-account risk [S20][S23]. Record the payer (student or parent) for refunds [S21]. *Removes a fraud-prone decision at stage 25.*
10. **Batch-aware residence card.** Show the university's group-filing dates or campus immigration day [S34][S35][S36], otherwise the HiKorea route, with the 90-day deadline. *Removes a decision at stage 40.*
11. **SIM sequencing.** Tell students that a travel eSIM cannot do bank or identity verification (excerpt [S41]) and what to switch to after the residence card. *Removes a tool switch at stages 37–38.*

**Remove errors and fraud (agency side)**

12. **Agency identity on every case, ready for the CoA field.** Korea will require agency details on the CoA (plan, H2-2026) and registration with missions [S3]. Capture the legal entity, registration status, responsible counselor and any sub-agent per case now, and let the university export it. *Removes a future compliance retrofit.*
13. **Flags with reasons, never auto-rejection.** Examples: the same lease template across students [S17], a degree from an unaccredited issuer [S15], a balance that appears just before the certificate date [S18], a test score not verifiable at the source [S54]. Follow the Element451 pattern: reasons plus a human decision [S74]. *Removes silent fraud passing at stage 9.*
14. **Authorship guard on writing.** Version history, rubric feedback and an applicant attestation. No generation. Cite Yonsei's "fail regardless of score" [S24] and KU's explanation demand [S19]. *Removes the ghostwriting risk at stage 13.*
15. **Standard-agreement disclosures per case.** Accurate program and work-rights information, refund process and amounts shown *before* payment [S6][S7]. Show it as soon as MOE publishes the text. *Removes "false or exaggerated advertising" exposure for agencies and universities.*

**Remove tool switching (operations)**

16. **Assignment, SLA and ageing as first-class fields.** The CRMs we found sell stages and checklists but not ageing [S49][S50]. A first-response clock, an "unassigned > N h" view and a stale-case view address the leakage they themselves describe [S50]. *Removes XL and memory.*
17. **Commission ledger tied to evidence.** Enrolment proof, tuition receipt and the deposit-paid date (agent lock) [S51] → expected commission → invoice → paid. Duplicate-claim detection [S51]. *Removes the university's Excel reconciliation and agency chasing.*
18. **University inbox → case.** Additional-document requests, CoA e-mails and results should land on the student's case, not in nine mailboxes [S23] or an agency inbox (**inference**). *Removes CS at stages 21–22 and 43.*
19. **Outcome metrics per agency, for the agency itself.** Visa refusal, arrival, registration and dropout — the measures Korea will apply to universities [S3][S5] and the UK proposes for agents [S72]. Define every metric and drill down to the cases (V8 rule). *Removes uncertainty for university partners.*

**Parents and sponsors**

20. **Sponsor view (consented, read-only):** what was paid, to whom, what is refundable and by when, the current step and the next deadline. No essay content (Yonsei PS rule [S24]). Nothing comparable exists in the market ([S58][S61][S62][S63]; Crimson summaries only [S76]). *Removes CS between family and agency at stage 45.*
