> **Headline changes since most public guides were written (2025–2026):**
> 1. International students in Korean higher education reached **307,464 (1 Apr 2026)**. **Vietnam (96,834) overtook China (78,890)** for the first time [S3][S4].
> 2. **Viet Nam became an Apostille Convention party on 11 Sep 2026** [S20][S21]. Before that date, Vietnamese documents needed Korean consular confirmation. The transition window matters for any application in flight.

> 4. The Ministry of Education (MOE) announced a "quality turn" on 8 Sep 2026. It includes a **university–agency standard agreement (표준협약서)**, to be distributed in H2/Oct 2026 and later made mandatory by law. The agreement covers accurate information, a ban on false advertising, and **tuition refunds paid directly to the student's own account** [S5][S6][S7].
> 5. MOE's standard guideline (rev. 2025-03-14) requires **tuition to be paid in the student's own name, e.g. through a personal virtual account, and refunded to the student's own account**. Group payment is allowed only by exception. It **forbids universities from admitting students in return for money or fees from agencies** [S8].

## 1. Korean study-abroad system overview

### 1.1 Program types
| Track | What it is | Visa | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Korean language program (어학연수) | University-affiliated Korean Language Institute (어학당). Usually 10-week terms, 4 intakes a year (verify per school). | **D-4-1** | MOE guideline says language institutes should be run directly by the university. For institutes run by outsourced operators, a visa may be refused [S8]. |
| Associate degree (전문학사) | 2–3-year junior colleges (전문대학) | D-2-1 | Korean requirement: TOPIK 2 to enter, TOPIK 3 before graduation (recommended standard) [S8] |
| Undergraduate freshman (학사 신입) | 4-year bachelor | D-2-2 | TOPIK 3 to enter, TOPIK 4 before graduation, or English-track equivalents [S8] |
| Undergraduate transfer (편입학) | Entry into year 2 or 3 | D-2-2 | Same language standard as freshmen. Needs the previous institution's graduation, completion or enrolment certificate plus transcripts [S8] |
| Master's (석사) | 2 years typical | D-2-3 | Graduate STEM programs may relax Korean requirements [S8] |
| Doctorate (박사) | 3+ years | D-2-4 | |
| Research / exchange / visiting | Non-degree | D-2-5 / D-2-6 / D-2-8 | See §4 |

### 1.2 Intakes and typical timeline
- **Two degree intakes:** Spring (March) and Fall (September). The standard MOE application form offers "1학기 (Spring) / 2학기 (Fall)" [S8].
- Example windows (these show how early deadlines fall, so do not hard-code them):
  - SNU 2027 Spring undergrad: online application 6–9 Jul 2026 [S40] [V]
  - SKKU 2027 Spring undergrad: 12–21 Aug 2026; graduate 11 Sep–9 Oct 2026 [S42] [V]
  - Hanyang application deadline 18 Sep 2026, documents by 30 Sep 2026 [S43] [V]
  - KNU 2026 academic year: undergrad online application 13–31 Oct 2025, final result 7 Jan 2026 [S47, via Korean consulate notice] [verify]
- **Rule of thumb:** application opens 6–9 months before the term → document screening → interview (online or in person) → result → tuition invoice and payment → **standard admission letter (표준입학허가서)** → optional CCVI → visa → entry → alien registration.
- Official entry flow per MOE [S8] [V]: 입학허가서 신청 → 표준입학허가서 발급 → (사증발급인정서 신청 → 발급) → 사증 신청 → 사증 발급 → 입국. The **university head, or a designated international-office head, may file the CCVI (사증발급인정서) application as the inviter**.

## 3. Admissions

### 3.1 Language requirements (MOE recommended standards, rev. 2025-03-14) [V] [S8]
- **4-year universities and graduate schools:** **TOPIK 3+ to enter, TOPIK 4+ before graduation.**
  - Admission at TOPIK 2 is allowed if the student completes **300+ hours of Korean training within the first year** (120 hours if they reach TOPIK 3 in the first semester).
  - 1+ year at the same university's language institute plus a school test at TOPIK-3 level is also accepted.
- **Junior colleges:** TOPIK 2 to enter, TOPIK 3 before graduation.
- **English track** (≥50% of graduation credits taught in English): **TOEFL iBT 59 / IELTS 5.5 / CEFR B2 / NEW TEPS 202** or equivalent. There is no graduation standard. Nationals of English-native or English-official-language countries are exempt with proof of their schooling.
- **Equivalents to TOPIK:** Ministry of Justice Social Integration Program (KIIP) and Sejong Institute courses. For example, TOPIK 3 = KIIP pre-test 61+ or KIIP level 3 completed, or Sejong Intermediate 1. Online Sejong certificates are **not** accepted.
- **Relaxations:** allowed for exchange students, government or foreign-government scholars, arts/sports, STEM graduate schools and bilingual programs. Universities may designate fewer than 20% of STEM departments with a TOPIK-3 graduation bar.
- **Visa-sensitive nationalities:** nationals of the **21 countries designated by the Minister of Justice (법무부장관 고시국가) and 5 "priority management" countries (중점관리국가)** entering "lower-tier" universities may face extra language requirements [S8]. The country lists are not in the documents we read [verify].
- **Tightening:** certified universities must have the share of students meeting the language standard (new students TOPIK 3, enrolled students TOPIK 4) at **40% in 2026 → 45% (2027) → 50% (2028) → 60% (2030)** [S6][S12]. Expect universities to become stricter on TOPIK.

### 3.2 Documents (typical)
From the MOE guideline [S8] and a sample university guide (SKKU, 2022 Spring) [S44]:
- Application form (university format; MOE sample form [S8]) and photo
- **Passport** copy, plus the parents' passports or IDs where the university checks the "both parents non-Korean" eligibility (SNU/Yonsei international tracks require that the student and both parents are non-Korean) [S40][S41]
- **Final-school diploma or graduation certificate and transcripts.** An "expected to graduate" certificate is acceptable, but the visa and entry are only possible in the final semester [S8].
- **Authentication:** **Apostille** (convention countries) **or Korean consular confirmation (영사확인)** at the Korean embassy/consulate in the issuing country (non-convention countries) [S44]
  - **China:** SKKU's 2022 guide still lists China as "without Apostille". China has been a party since 7 Nov 2023, so older guides are stale.
  - Chinese applicants have also been asked for **CHSI/CDGDC verification reports** (e.g. the "Verification Report of China Secondary Education Qualification Certificate") and **Huikao results** [S44]. Check each university's current guide.
- **Family relationship documents:** e.g. a family relationship certificate, or **Hukou (户口簿) for Chinese applicants**, used to prove parentage for the eligibility check and for a parent acting as financial sponsor. A one-parent family must add proof of marital status [S44].
- **Proof of funds** [S8]: a bank balance certificate in the name of the student or a sponsor, **issued within 30 days of application**. If the certificate states a validity date it is honoured until then, capped at 6 months from issue. Alternatively, a domestic remittance certificate. A sponsor also provides an employment certificate or business registration, an asset certificate, and a pledge to pay study costs. Scholars submit scholarship certificates; exchange students submit an invitation plus a tuition-waiver letter.
- **Personal statement / study plan; recommendation letters.** The MOE sample list has 2 letters. SNU requires a Personal Statement & Study Plan and recommendation letters [S8][S40].
- TOPIK or English score report; portfolio for arts
- **Translations:** documents not in Korean or English must be translated and notarized (e.g. PNU) [S45] [verify]

### 3.3 Fees, interviews, admission letter
- **Application fee:** set by each university and typically non-refundable. SKKU 2022: **KRW 100,000** [S44]. A commercial source says KRW 50,000–150,000 is typical [verify].
- **Interviews:** common (online or in person). They are also used to assess Korean where no TOPIK score is given [S44].
- **Tuition payment → Certificate of Admission (표준입학허가서):** the student pays the tuition invoice, typically by wire to a **student-specific virtual account** [S44]. The university then issues the **standard admission letter**, which must follow the Ministry of Justice FIMS form [S8].
  - MOE requires that tuition is **paid in the student's name and refunded to the student's account**. Group payment or refund needs proof that it was unavoidable [S8].
  - MOE also tells universities to **avoid requiring deposits** at admission and to **avoid blanket tuition discounts** at the recruitment stage [S8].
- **Selection must be done by the university itself.** Universities are **prohibited from admitting students in exchange for money or fees from agencies** [S8].

### 3.4 Application platforms used by universities
- **UwayApply (uwayapply.com)**: used by "more than 300 institutions in South Korea, the majority of universities and language schools" (Flywire partnership page) [S30]. Kyung Hee uses it [S46]. SKKU and Kookmin guides are hosted on it [S44].
- **JinhakApply (jinhakapply.com)**: Hanyang's undergraduate and graduate international applications [S43]; KNU's international application [S47].
- University-own systems and the Study in Korea portal.

  - **Certified (181 degree / 123 language):** standard admission letter alone, **except nationals of the Minister-designated and priority-management countries, who must still submit financial proof**.
  - **General (134):** full review.
  - **Visa-restricted "precision screening" (16 degree / 4 language):** visa issuance in principle **banned for 1 year from Fall 2026**.
  - Names of general and consulting universities are not published.
- Search results claimed a "50% financial relaxation for regional universities via e-application" [verify, not found in the primary text we read].

### 4.3 CCVI (사증발급인정서) vs applying at the embassy
- **CCVI:** the inviting university (the president or the designated international-office head) applies in Korea on the student's behalf, via the Visa Portal or Immigration. The student then gets the visa at the mission using the CCVI number, or via e-visa [S8][S14] [V].
- **Direct embassy application:** the student applies at the Korean mission with the full document set. Missions set local extras such as interviews, deposit holding periods and consular-confirmed documents [S26].
- **Product note:** CCVI shifts document collection to the university. Hangyul can pre-assemble a university-ready CCVI packet.

### 4.4 After entry
- **Alien registration (residence card, 외국인등록증):** within **90 days of entry** at the local immigration office. **Address change must be reported within 14 days** (HiKorea, community centre or immigration office) [S27] [verify on HiKorea].
- **Part-time work (시간제 취업).** It is "체류자격외활동" and needs **prior permission**. The university confirms language level and academic standing [S24] [V: rule exists].
  - **Eligibility:** D-2-1–4, 6, 7 immediately; **D-2-8 and D-4-1/D-4-7 only after 6 months** [S24].
  - **Weekly weekday hours** (regime since Jul 2023) [S24][S28] [verify, sources partly garbled or commercial]:

    | Program | Language standard | Standard not met | Standard met | Preferential* |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | Associate; Bachelor Y1–2 | TOPIK 3 | 10 h | 25 h | 30 h |
    | Bachelor Y3–4 | TOPIK 4 | 10 h | 25 h | 30 h |
    | Master's / PhD | TOPIK 4 | 15 h | 30 h | 35 h |
    | D-4 language trainee | TOPIK 2 | 10 h | 20 h | 25 h |

    \* Preferential = certified university / good grades / high Korean.

  - Degree students who meet the language standard reportedly have **no cap on weekends, public holidays and vacations** [verify].
  - Barred sectors include construction and "special-type workers" such as delivery. Manufacturing is barred unless TOPIK 4+ [S24] [verify].
  - Penalties: up to 3 years' imprisonment or a KRW 30M fine, and deportation [S24].
  - **Policy in motion (2026):** the Ministry of Justice is reportedly reviewing a move to **exempt students who meet the language standard from separate permission** [S29, news] [verify].
- **Extension (체류기간 연장):** apply before expiry, online at HiKorea or in person, with an enrolment certificate, transcript, tuition receipt and financial proof. The certification status of the university affects screening [verify details].
- **D-10 job seeking after graduation:** graduates can change status to D-10 (job seeking or start-up preparation). D-10 holders doing an internship must report within 15 days [S27] [verify timing and points system on HiKorea].
- **Study Korea 300K Project** (announced 16 Aug 2023) [S9][S31]:
  - Target: **300,000 international students by 2027** (220k degree + 80k non-degree per ICEF/ACA [verify]).
  - GKS STEM expansion; regional "specialized zones"; linking recruitment → education → regional employment.
  - **The target was reached in 2026, one year early.** In Sep 2026 MOE announced the numeric target is being replaced by quality measures [S5][S6]:
    - agency standard agreements
    - credential verification
    - Higher Education Act amendment to make recruitment and management a university duty
    - recruitment limits on universities in financial crisis or failing accreditation, from the 5th cycle (2029+)
    - native-language 24h AI chatbot and job matching
    - a "regional talent" certification from 2027
    - GKS engineering share to 45%

## 6. Settlement (post-arrival)

1. **Arrival.** Airport pickup and temporary housing are commonly sold by agencies (HAH Seoul lists pickup, housing, SIM/T-money and ARC help) [S34].
2. **SIM.** Prepaid on arrival with a passport; postpaid usually after the ARC [verify].
3. **Alien registration** within 90 days (immigration visit reservation via HiKorea) [S27].
4. **Bank account.** Most banks require an ARC. New accounts often open as **한도제한계좌 (limited accounts)** with low daily transfer and withdrawal limits until the account's purpose is documented. Students may show an enrolment certificate. Foreigners without documented income face outward remittance limits (reported USD 5,000 per transaction and USD 50,000 per year) [S35] [verify: commercial/InvestKorea].
5. **NHIS.** Automatic enrollment for D-2 from entry / alien registration. Monthly premium (2026 student rate KRW 79,320 per university notice) is due by the 25th. **Arrears can block visa extension** [S17][S18]. Students not covered by NHIS (e.g. certain categories) must hold equivalent private insurance per MOE [S8].
6. **Dormitories.**
   - MOE asks universities to secure dorm places prioritised for foreigners, or to allocate a share [S8].
   - Examples: KNU gives a **full first-semester dorm fee subsidy** (an "excellent certified" university case) [S12]; SKKU has a dorm portal (dorm.skku.edu) [S42]; Kyung Hee lists residence halls on both campuses [S46].
7. **Legal orientation.** Universities must run ≥1 hour a year of Korean law education, covering immigration, drugs, voice-phishing and sexual-violence law [S8]. This is a natural Hangyul content module.
8. **Address change** within 14 days. **Part-time permission** before any job. **Extension** before expiry. **D-10** at graduation.

## 9. Opportunities for Hangyul Univ

1. **Eligibility engine.** Match the student profile to program rules:
   - nationality and parents' nationality
   - years of schooling (12-year rule; the MOE equivalence tables for 11-year systems [S8])
   - TOPIK/IELTS vs track
   - Minister-designated / priority-country financial rules
   - the university's certification tier, which drives visa difficulty
2. **Time-versioned rule and data registry** (`research-sources.json` is the seed). Every rule (apostille status, funding thresholds, part-time hours, deadlines) carries `source_url`, `verified_at` and `effective_from`, with automatic staleness flags. Examples of stale data today: SKKU's guide "China non-apostille" and Vietnam's Sep 2026 switch.
3. **Document vault and reuse.** Upload once, reuse across universities, the visa and the extension. Track **bank-certificate expiry (30 days / max 6 months)**, **TOPIK validity (2 years)** and apostille vs consular path per document. Add an OCR checklist and translation/notarization flags.
4. **Apostille path guidance** per issuing country and issue date, with embassy-specific instructions (e.g. Nepal and Myanmar consular confirmation; Vietnam transition).
5. **Agency CRM designed around the MOE standard agreement:**
   - agreement templates
   - an audit trail of the information given to students (anti-false-advertising)
   - refund-to-student-account enforcement
   - an agency quality score based on dropout, illegal stay and visa refusal
6. **University dashboard and outcome analytics** that help universities hit certification KPIs:
   - illegal-stay rate
   - dropout rate
   - share meeting TOPIK 3/4, rising 40→60%
   - insurance enrollment
   - law-education completion
   - nationality diversity (one "excellent" criterion: a single nationality ≤50%)

   This is the university's real pain point and the wedge for selling to them.
7. **Payments** that **never pool tuition.** Deep-link to the university's student-named virtual account or Flywire/Uway rails. Hangyul charges only its own service fees.
8. **Post-arrival lifecycle:** ARC in 90 days, NHIS payment reminders, part-time permission eligibility calculator (hours by TOPIK/year), address-change reminder in 14 days, extension, D-10 transition, job matching.
9. **CCVI packet builder** for universities that apply for CCVI on students' behalf.

## 10. Assumptions and items requiring legal / operational verification

1. **Agency licensing (유학알선업).** We found no current Korean registration or licensing regime specific to inbound-student recruitment agencies. The (outbound) "해외유학알선업" registration regime historically existed and was later deregulated [verify with counsel]. MOE now plans a **standard university–agency agreement (H2/Oct 2026)** and **legal amendments to make key terms mandatory** [S5][S6][S7]. Track the Higher Education Act amendment.
2. **Commissions and recruitment fees.**
   - The MOE guideline prohibits universities **receiving** money or fees from agencies for admitting students [S8].
   - Whether universities may **pay** per-student commissions to agencies or platforms, and how the standard agreement treats that, is **unverified**.
   - Also check agencies charging students, and the Fair Trade Act / Terms Act on refund clauses (MOE cites the 약관규제법 for language-institute refunds) [S8].
3. **PIPA (개인정보 보호법).**
   - Cross-border transfer (Art. 28-8, in force 15 Sep 2023) needs a lawful basis such as **separate consent**, with disclosure of recipient, purpose, retention and how to refuse [S39].
   - Hangyul will move passports, family records (Hukou) and bank statements between countries and to agencies. Plan: separate consent flows, a processor/entrustment registry, and handling of sensitive and unique identifiers (passport and ARC numbers are 고유식별정보; check the Art. 24 rules).
   - Also check the laws of the origin countries (e.g. China PIPL, Vietnam Decree 13/2023 and its successor law) [verify].
4. **Education Internationalization Capability Certification (교육국제화역량 인증제, IEQAS).**
   - The university's tier drives visa screening.
   - The ministries sanction universities for poor recruitment (visa bans up to 3 years, "one-strike-out") [S12].
   - Universities will therefore be **selective about agencies**. Hangyul should expose agency-level outcome data and compliance evidence.
   - Watch the 5th-cycle (2029+) criteria.
5. **Payments / PG.**
   - Collecting tuition on behalf of students conflicts with MOE's student-name payment rule [S8].
   - Hangyul's own fees raise questions on cross-border card acceptance, foreign-exchange reporting (외국환거래법) and possible 전자금융업 / PG registration if funds are held [verify with counsel].
6. **Visa facts to re-verify on HiKorea / Immigration manuals** (the Immigration "외국인 유학생 사증발급 및 체류관리 지침" is updated frequently):
   - exact D-2 sub-type names
   - part-time hour table and permission fee
   - financial thresholds and embassy deposit periods
   - D-10 application timing and points
   - the 21 Minister-designated and 5 priority countries
7. **NHIS premium** amount and student reduction rate: only a university notice was read. Confirm on nhis.or.kr.
8. **Competitor claims** (Welko partner count, Shinhwa booths, DreamWay success rates) are self-reported.
9. **University admissions pages** change URLs every cycle. Store both the stable landing page and the per-cycle guide URL.
