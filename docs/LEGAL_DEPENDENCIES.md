# Legal dependencies of Hangyul Univ (V9)

*2026-10-14. This document lists **product** dependencies on legal decisions. It contains no legal policy and no legal advice; every text shown to users (terms, privacy notice, consent wording, retention periods, refund terms) remains **subject to approval by qualified counsel** and, where applicable, to the Korean authorities named below. "Product today" states what the code does so counsel can see exactly what is being approved. Sources: `docs/research/v9-operating-model.md` (2026-10-10), `docs/RESEARCH.md`, `docs/SECURITY.md`, `docs/SUBMISSION_CONNECTORS.md`.*

Legend — **Product today**: implemented behaviour. **Needs decision**: what counsel/business must decide before launch. **Product change when decided**: what engineering does once it knows. **Status**: `OPEN` (no decision exists), `DRAFT` (placeholder text exists), `ANNOUNCED` (a regulator has announced a rule but the text/effective date is not final).
