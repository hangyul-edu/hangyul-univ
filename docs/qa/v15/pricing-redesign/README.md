# V15 b — homepage pricing section, before and after

Captured against the running marketing app at `localhost:3100`, Korean, at the six widths the brief names.
`REPORT.md` §129 is the written pass; this folder is what it is written about.

| Folder | What is in it |
|---|---|
| `before/` | the V15 a section — three cards, the bordered table, the two exclusion paragraphs |
| `after/` | the recomposed section |
| `locales/` | `ar` (RTL), `de` (the longest labels), `vi`, at 390 and 1440, comparison open |
| `a11y/` | reduced motion, the recommended plan's focus ring, the phone tablist under keyboard focus |

Per width: `-collapsed`, `-expanded`; plus `-hover` and `-focus` at 1024 and above, `-firstview` below
500 (what a phone shows when the section comes into view — the recommended plan, whole, before Basic),
and `1440-to-faq` / `390-to-faq` for the join into the next section.

**One difference between the two sets.** The `before/` shots were taken with the floating pill header
visible, so it overlaps the section heading in a few of them; `after/` hides it for the capture, because
the heading's composition is part of what changed and could not be reviewed underneath it. The header
itself is unchanged by this pass.
