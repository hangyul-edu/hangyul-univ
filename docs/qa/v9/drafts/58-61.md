# 58. V9 Student Experience

*Written before any V9 code change. Persona used throughout: Aarati's situation without her advantages — 19, Kathmandu, budget Android phone (360–390 px), limited English, **no TOPIK**, parents pay. "PRIMARY QUESTION" is written for every major page before it is touched; a page with two unrelated questions is split or recomposed.*

## 58.1 What a zero-knowledge student actually meets today (verified 2026-10-10)

| Moment | What the product does | Why it fails the job |
|---|---|---|
| Quick check: Nepal · Bachelor's · "Neither yet" | `POST /public/quick-eligibility` → **0 programs**, 31 "not yet", "21 more with TOPIK 3" | The honest answer to "Can I realistically study in Korea?" is **yes — through a Korean language program first** (10 language-institute programs in the catalogue accept her today; that is the route most students without TOPIK actually take) or an English-taught track with IELTS. The product answers "no" and stops. |
| Budget | First-year range, cash before arrival; insurance and visa fee "no reliable figure yet" | The researched visa fee, residence-card fee (KRW 35,000, official) and the **funds the family must show for the visa** (e.g. Myanmar KRW 20,000,000 for Seoul, 6-month history, certificate ≤ 30 days old — already in the visa route data) never reach the money screens. The parent's biggest money question — "how much must be in our account, for how long?" — has no answer before the visa stage. No line says who receives the money, when, or whether it is refundable. |
| Discovery "For you" | Tier tabs with counts including **zeros** ("Needs your grades 0", "Not eligible yet 0") | Zero tabs are noise; rows don't show the scholarship or "what would unlock this" until expanded. |
| Documents | Requirement-centric dossier, reuse per application | Nothing tells the student **who can see a passport or bank certificate, which university received it, how long it is kept, or how to withdraw sharing** — the privacy questions the brief lists. The data exists (`applications[].sent`, consent records). |
| Parents | No sponsor surface (V8 decided "not built") | Families fund and decide; today the student screenshots the app or forwards KakaoTalk messages. |
| Language | `vi/ne/my` student 100 %, but V5 namespaces (dossier 87–91 %, success 62 %, writing 24 %, concierge 67 %, visaroute 90 %); 38 locales have **no** student namespace; ja 71 %, ru 57 %, zh-Hant 66 %; th, id, hi, bn, ur 0 % | A Burmese or Nepali student meets English on the writing, concierge and success screens; a Thai, Indonesian, Hindi, Bengali or Urdu student gets an English app with a notice. |

## 58.4 Localization target

A locale counts as **complete for students** only when (1) every key of the student-journey manifest (keys reachable from student web screens, server-sent codes included) is translated, (2) the leak scan finds no unexplained English on the core screens for that locale, (3) a synthetic review scored ≥ 4/5 on every dimension for the four reader profiles (18–20 student, 23–29 graduate, 35–49 parent, 50–65 parent). Locales that do not meet it are not offered as a student-app language; they are offered on the public site only, and the picker says so. Priority locales first: ko, en, vi, ne, my, uz, mn, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, ja, th, id, hi, bn, ur, ar, ru — plus kk, ky, fil (visa-priority countries).

# 59. V9 Agency Operating System

*Persona: a counselor with 200 students starting at 9 AM; a manager who must answer six questions in 60 seconds.*

## 59.1 Verified gaps

| Area | Today (2026-10-10) | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Waiting on | Server values `us / student / university / external` (none) | "Us" hides **who**: the named counselor, the agency team (unassigned review), or Hangyul (assignment, payment, ops). The brief's model is Student · Counselor · Agency · Hangyul · University · External · No one, with one meaning everywhere. |
| Work queue | Today = case ranking with reasons (`/agency/today/queue`) **plus** eight category counts (`/agency/today`) | Two ways to see the same work; no filter by work type on the ranked list. |
| Team | `/agency/team`: name, role, title, languages, active | No countries, university or degree/major expertise per person, no personal capacity, no availability (leave), no weighted workload or review load; capacity is one agency-wide number (60) split evenly. |
| Templates | 12 templates, all ORG scope, en/ko only, kinds without payment/arrival/housing | A Vietnamese agency has no Vietnamese template; no personal/team scope in practice; no permission rule for editing organisation templates. |
| Providers / Services | V5 queue and providers list | Not operational: no per-provider open cases, SLA, last issue, agreement status as the first fact. |
| Submissions | V6 "Ready to submit" for authorised fixture | Not a queue across all methods: Ready · Needs final review · Authorized · Submitted · Confirmation missing · University received · Issue. |
| Consultations | Tasks view "Meetings" | A meeting's notes, decisions, follow-up tasks and next appointment are not one record in the student workspace. |
| Manager | `/agency/ops`: backlog, ageing, load, SLA, deadlines, stages | "Oldest blocked case" and "unassigned paid case" not first-class; load ignores reviews, visa and service cases. |
| Quality | Outcome metrics (V5) on Performance | Not organised as process quality (correction loops, revision loops, completeness at submission, university and visa extra-document rates, turnaround, student response time, arrival issues) with cohort and n. |
| Messages | Channel model; in-app only | No per-message delivery state shown; messages cannot be tied to an application or task from the thread. |
