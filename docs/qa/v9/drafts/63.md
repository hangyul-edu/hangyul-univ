## 63.3 Localisation

The V9 gate: a locale is offered in the **student app** only when every key of the student-journey manifest is translated **and** a recorded leak scan found 0 English leaks on ~166 page views (390 + 1440, 10 personas). Namespace-level coverage in `npm run i18n:check` stays below 100 % for these locales by design — it counts staff-only subtrees (agency items, reviewer workspace, concierge staff) that the student app never renders and the manifest excludes.

| Locale | Student manifest | Leak scan | Synthetic review (min of 20 scores) |
|---|---|---|---|
| en | source | — | — |
| ko, vi, ne, my, uz, mn, ja, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, ar, ru, kk, ky, tr, th, id, hi, bn, ur, fil | 100 % | see §63.3 table in `packages/i18n/locales/_student_status.json` | 4 / 5 |

**No locale is HUMAN_REVIEWED.** Each `docs/qa/v9/l10n/<loc>.md` carries the line "synthetic review by an AI model — not a human review" and a residual-risk list. The other 35 locales keep the marketing site and are **not** offered as a student-app language; the picker says so.

Terminology fixed across locales during the pass: `verdict.COMPETITIVE`/`REACH` were swapped or odds-worded in nine locales; English `common:tier` itself still said "Competitive match"/"Reach"; Korean "Possible match" had become "there is a chance". All now follow STATE_MAP.

## 63.5 Not re-measured in V9 (and therefore not claimed)

Accessibility (axe), the full Playwright E2E suite, the 299-view visual QA sweep, production LCP and the Android smoke were **not re-run in this pass** on this machine; the V8 results stand as the last measurement (axe 0 violations on 57 routes × 1280/390; E2E 47/47 deterministic; LCP 1.85–2.05 s phone). V9 changed the shells, the student money/visa/sponsor screens and every console page, so these must be re-run before launch; they are listed in §64 under human validation and infrastructure. The blind visual test's human round has not run either.
