# 64. V9 Launch Readiness & Remaining Dependencies

*Every remaining item is classified by what it needs. "CODE-COMPLETE" means the product behaves correctly and truthfully today and needs nothing from engineering; everything else names the outside thing it waits for. The same classification is generated from real settings and records on `/hq/readiness` (seven categories), so operations sees the same list. Legal detail: `docs/LEGAL_DEPENDENCIES.md`. Research protocols: `docs/research/v9-validation-protocols.md`.*

## 64.1 By category

### CODE-COMPLETE (works now; no external dependency)
One profile and dossier with reuse and per-application applicability · language routes · one money plan (payee, timing, refundability, basis) and money-to-show · sponsor view · document visibility · strategy, portfolio and writing review with the quality gate · guided submission with evidence and the seven-state submissions pipeline · visa route resolution for 14 countries from researched playbooks · arrival/housing/pickup/settlement service cases · one waiting-on model across the three consoles and the student app · agency one work queue, team weights and leave/reassignment, templates with language variants, process quality · university evidence-first review, review-and-advance, safe bulk with select-all-matching, questions-first analytics with drill-down, rule impact preview, partnership value · HQ failure list, partner quality, readiness page · adversarial recovery for 18 scenarios · the localisation gate and 20 locales' student journey.

### NEEDS PROVIDER AGREEMENT (a contract, not just a key)
WhatsApp / KakaoTalk / Zalo business messaging · payment providers (Stripe/Toss merchant account; the V9 payment callback endpoint exists and is idempotent but returns 503 until a provider is configured) · housing inventory (no listing is invented) · airport transfer providers · SIM/eSIM, insurance and relocation partners · **submission rails**: UWAY, JinhakApply, Gradnet and Study in Korea have no public API, agency account, batch upload or status callback (re-checked 2026-10-10) — watch NIIED's announced one-stop application and the MOJ's FIMS plans.

### NEEDS BUSINESS DATA
Real service prices (demo prices are flagged) · verified catalogue figures (tuition, fees, deadlines, dorm costs are sample data with `verification_status=DEMO`) · real partner contracts (no university or agency agreement is recorded; "partner" is never shown without one) · university refund rules (the refund case says "your counselor confirms") · university visa-certification tiers (unknown everywhere, so no e-visa is ever offered) · Hangyul's own photography (licensed CC0/CC BY images are used, and the Eduan template photographs have no licence recorded in the package).

### NEEDS LEGAL APPROVAL
Terms, privacy notice and consent wording · retention schedule (the product says "policy pending approval") · cross-border transfer mechanism · recruitment-fee and refund terms · agency submission authority · sponsor-access basis · MOE standard university–agency agreement (announced 2026-09-08, text not public) · MOJ draft rules (agency details on the Certificate of Admission, overseas agency registration, university visa-refusal scoring — drafts with target dates, shown nowhere as current law). Full table: `docs/LEGAL_DEPENDENCIES.md` (L1–L13).

### NEEDS HUMAN REVIEW
**No locale is HUMAN_REVIEWED** — every record says "synthetic review by an AI model — not a human review". Human translation review per locale (protocol §7) · the residual-risk lists in each `docs/qa/v9/l10n/<loc>.md` (certification tier names, Korean administrative terms, parent-facing money and visa sentences) · 14 visa fields and 9 arrival facts that need a call or email to a post (listed in `VISA_ROUTES.md` / `ARRIVAL_SERVICES.md`) · the blind visual test's human round.

### NEEDS HUMAN VALIDATION (users)
No real student, parent, counselor, manager or admissions officer has used V9. Protocols and recruitment criteria are written for 5–8 students, 3–5 parents, 5 counselors, 3 managers, 5 university admissions staff and 3 university managers (`docs/research/v9-validation-protocols.md`). **Every V9 task number is a scripted walkthrough, not an observed user.**

### NEEDS INFRASTRUCTURE
Production deployment (none exists) · TLS/HSTS/CSP · rate limiting and lockout · MFA for staff · scheduled-jobs worker (daily job runs manually; scheduled exports are shown disabled) · backups and retention jobs · penetration test · iOS build machine (iOS has never been run).

## 64.2 Launch blockers, shortest list

| Kind | Blocker |
|---|---|
| **CODE** | None known for the journeys V9 covers. Open engineering items are improvements, not blockers: the Kyrgyzstan 2027-01-01 document/language changes need the loader re-run on/after that date; JPEG/WEBP truncation is undetected; a housing case returned to the team after a provider cancellation is not listed on the Providers page; the university list's eligibility summary judges submitted applicants against the current published minimum; ~35 locales have no student journey; agency mobile does not yet show the V9 queue. |

| **HUMAN VALIDATION** | All usability rounds, human translation review, the human blind test, and the visa/arrival fields that need confirmation from a post. |

## 64.3 What must not happen before those are closed

Do not enable `AUTHORIZED_AGENCY` for a real university, do not label a provider or university a "partner", do not take real money, do not promise a retention period, do not mark a locale HUMAN_REVIEWED, and do not describe any V9 measurement as user-validated.
