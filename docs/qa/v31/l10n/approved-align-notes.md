## Meaning deltas draft -> approved (applied in every locale)

- **all_in_one.f2 / premium.f2**: the draft said "we manage ... *with you*" (shared management). Approved 대학 지원 최대 N곳 관리 = up to N applications managed (by us). Removed "with you"/"together"; kept each locale's voice (active "we manage" or passive).
- **all_in_one.f5**: the draft said *help with* preparing visa documents (비자 신청 서류 준비 지원). Approved 비자 서류 준비 = visa document preparation. Replaced "help/support with preparing" with "preparation of visa documents". Left the dormitory half as it was (the same in draft and approved).
- **premium.f4**: the draft added *documents* and "key dates" (비자 서류·주요 일정 집중 관리). Approved 비자 일정 집중 관리 = intensive management of the visa schedule only. Removed "documents". "Key dates" became visa schedule/timeline. Left the dormitory half unchanged.
- **basic.f1**: the draft gave the diagnostic consultation an explicit count (진단 상담 1회). Approved: the consultation has no count, plus 1 follow-up. Removed the explicit "1" from the diagnostic consultation where a translation had it (bn, my, vi, zh-Hans). In every locale "1 follow-up" stays explicit.
- No meaning delta, left as `current`: all positions (approved KO includes 지원부터 / application, so "from application" in premium.position is correct), all_in_one.f1/f3/f4, basic.f2-f5, premium.f1/f3/f5 (draft-EN differences there are stylistic. ## Unsure / for the owner

- **ar, digit check**: 4 strings fail the plain digit check but are exact: all_in_one.f2 `طلبَي` and all_in_one.f3 `جولتَي` are grammatical duals (= exactly 2), and basic.f1 / premium.f2 `واحدة` = one. This is the reviewed `current` wording. I left it alone, because writing "2" before a dual noun is ungrammatical in Arabic. If digits are mandatory, use `مقابلة تجريبية (1)`-style forms.
- **basic.f1, articles**: de/es/fr/it/nl/sv/el/pt/ro keep "a/one" (Ein/Una/Une/Een/Ett/Μια/O) before the diagnostic consultation. I read this as an article, not a count, so I left it. Change it if "one" could be read as a contractual cap.
- **basic.f4**: `current` follows the draft-EN "checklist of the documents you need" in most locales. Approved says 기본 입학 서류 체크리스트 (basic *admission* document checklist). I treated this as stylistic and did not edit it. Strictly, "documents you need" is slightly broader than admission documents. uz and vi already say admission documents.
- **basic.f3 sr** (`на једном месту`, "in one place") and **ur** (`ایک ساتھ`, "together") do not say "compare" outright. I left them as reviewed.
- **premium.f4**: most locales render 집중 as "closely" (eng/nauw/de près/ใกล้ชิด) rather than "intensive". I kept that register from `current`. ar now uses مكثفة (intensive), following pre_v31.
- **all_in_one.f5 / premium.f4 second halves** (dormitory) were left as reviewed. Some read "application dates" where approved_en says "deadlines". 일정 covers both.
